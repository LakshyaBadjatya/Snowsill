# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple: Snowsill now opens without any security warning.
- Installed copies update to this version automatically.

## 1.0.0

First release of Snowsill, developed by Lakshya Badjatya.

- Snow that falls over your desktop and piles up on window tops, the Dock and desktop widgets
- Push snow with your pointer; drag a window and its snow tumbles off
- Icicles under windows, a snowman in deep snow, frosty corners in a long blizzard
- The snow plow: call it or let it come by itself; it clears the bottom of the screen and drives off
- Rain and fog, or match the live weather in your city
- Desktop-only mode, apps kept snow-free, one switch per display, schedule, ⌃⌥⌘S shortcut
- Automatic updates
